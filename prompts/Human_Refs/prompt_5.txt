Anomaly Summary
Downlink throughput and scheduling stay consistently high across the window, with radio quality holding steady (CQI/MCS flat). 
That looks like a busy but healthy session rather than a congested or blocked one.
Retransmissions are elevated on both links, but they rise alongside high tx volume and do not bring errors with them.
Traffic is balanced UL↔DL and stable over time (no bursty spikes or step-changes). We’re not seeing the asymmetry or volatility typical of volumetric abuse.
LIME/SHAP agree the decision is driven primarily by sustained high downlink bitrate pushing toward Normal, with tx/re-tx acting as secondary, countervailing signals.

Misclassification Likelihood
Low likelihood of a missed DDoS. 
In this dataset, attacks trend toward suppressed DL throughput and collateral symptoms (error growth, erratic scheduling). 

Mitigation Step
Track retransmissions over the next hour; alert only if they rise with falling bitrate or growing error counters (tighten the composite rule, not the single metric)
Correlate with scheduler/backhaul stats to confirm the re-tx uptick is load-related (e.g., momentary PRB contention) rather than packet loss.
