Anomaly Summary
The radio link looks unhealthy: CQI/MCS sit low and stay flat, so the air interface isn’t negotiating robust modulation.
Retransmissions are consistently elevated on both uplink and downlink across the window.
Downlink throughput is depressed while uplink activity remains comparatively busy, giving a UL-heavy, DL-starved pattern.
LIME/SHAP both lean on low downlink bitrate, high re-tx, and low DL MCS as the main drivers behind the model’s Anomalous call.

Misclassification Likelihood
Low.

Mitigation Steps
Pull cell/sector RF KPIs for this interval (CQI/MCS trends, interference, PRB/scheduler metrics) and check neighbor/handover behavior to confirm a radio problem; remediate if persistent.
Tighten post-processing: gate DDoS alerts when “low DL bitrate + low CQI/MCS + high re-tx” co-occur; only escalate if DL volume or control-plane anomalies rise with it.
