Anomaly Summary
Sustained UL push: Uplink bitrate and packet volume stay high across all windows, with elevated UL retransmissions.
Asymmetric traffic: Downlink remains comparatively quiet while RF quality and MCS/CQI are healthy, so the imbalance isn’t explained by poor radio.
Explainability view: SHAP highlights UL bitrate/UL & DL tx (plus some DL bitrate influence) as the main drivers; LIME mostly agrees on the throughput/volume story despite minor threshold quirks
No error accumulation: Error counters are essentially clean, which points to an egress-heavy workload or outbound flood rather than congestion or link impairment.

Misclassification Likelihoo
Moderate false-positive risk.  
Could be legitimate UL-heavy activit since RF is good and errors are low; the model likely keys on the persistent UL load and retransmission levels.

Mitigation Step
Pin down top talkers and destinations on the affected UE/sector; if you see small, repetitive packets or wide fan-out, apply egress rate-limits or temporary ACLs.
If the pattern persists post-shaping, notify/quarantine the source UE and check for backhaul policing conflicts that could be amplifying retries.
